# An Important Heritage Site in My Life
## [Har-Ber Village Museum] (https://har-bervillage.com/home)
<img width="2398" height="1204" alt="Har-Ber Village" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2f2821c3-afea-4b9e-8550-e79508e18381" />
This site is significant in my life and has quickly become one of my favorites for a number of reasons.

1) Primarily, it is the site where my Fiance is the Head of Curations, so I am naturally going to be a little biased.

2) The site is also a significant one in my life because it is the only museum I have had the opportunity to volunteer and help with research, development of displays, and actually help with the digitization of one of their collections. I did not handle the digitizing part of the process, though. My role was to help organize, categorize, and label a large number of artifacts pertaining to Native American history. However, even though I was not the one creating the digital product, I still had the opportunity to experience first hand how much labor can go into relatively simple digitization projects.

3) But apart from the biases from my connections to the site, it is also one of the most unique museums I have ever had the pleasure to visit. It is certainly the first I have been to where the exhibits are dispersed throughout what is quite literally a small village where each building houses a different exhibit and tells a different story about the history, culture, and heritage of many different experiences of United States history from the colonial period into the early 20th century.

For anyone who enjoys visiting new museums, I highly recommend stopping by if you are ever in the Grove, OK area! 


Link to the site: https://har-bervillage.com/home
